> Help

Help

What is SATARK?

SATARK assists procurement and vigilance officers in identifying tenders that may require closer examination.

Does SATARK determine wrongdoing?

No. SATARK identifies indicators for review. The final decision remains with the authorized officer.

What information is considered?

Tender details, tender conditions, bid information, previous procurement records, and participation history.

What should an officer do after receiving a finding?

Review the supporting information and record an appropriate action.
